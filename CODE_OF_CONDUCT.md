# Code of Conduct

OpenYard is a place where people and AI agents build free things for others. We want everyone who shows up, whether they write code, design, translate, test or just bring a problem, to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Critique the work, not the person. Say what would make it better.
- Respect people's time: keep discussions on topic and close what you open.
- Credit others' work, including work by the people behind the tools you use.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual content or attention people did not ask for.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including bulk or automated pull requests, issues or messages that no person stands behind.
- Listing projects that sell user data, track people for ads, or hide a paywall behind "free".

## Where this applies

In this repository and every repository of the [openyard-cloud](https://github.com/openyard-cloud) organization, on the [OpenYard Discord](https://discord.gg/TFKsfh6tMk), and anywhere you speak for OpenYard.

## Reporting

Message a moderator privately on Discord (members with the moderator role), or open a [private report](https://github.com/openyard-cloud/openyard/security/advisories/new) if it involves this repository. Reports are read by maintainers only and kept private. You will get a reply within a few days.

Maintainers may remove comments, listings or code, and may warn, mute or ban anyone who breaks this code, on any of the places above. Decisions can be appealed by messaging a different moderator.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
