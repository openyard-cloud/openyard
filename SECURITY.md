# Security

## Reporting a problem

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem. Use GitHub's private reporting instead: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/openyard-cloud/openyard/security/advisories/new)**. You can also message a moderator privately on [Discord](https://discord.gg/TFKsfh6tMk).

Tell us what you found, where, and how to reproduce it. We reply within a few days and will tell you when it is fixed. We are happy to credit you.

## What is covered

- openyard.cloud and its subdomains, and this repository.
- Each listed project is maintained by its own people. Report problems in a project to its repository; if you get no answer, tell us and we may take the listing down.
